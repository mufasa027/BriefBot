📰 Russian strikes on Kyiv kill at least nine, Ukraine officials say

Russian forces have escalated attacks on Kyiv with ballistic missile strikes that killed at least nine people, according to Ukrainian officials. The assault on the capital represents a significant intensification of Moscow's offensive, marking one of the deadliest attacks on the city in recent weeks. This development occurs amidst ongoing heavy fighting in eastern Ukraine and underscores the continued severity of the conflict.
