# Privacy Policy for CipherBrief Newsroom

**Effective Date: August 8, 2026**

## 1. Introduction
CipherBrief Newsroom ("we", "our", or "us") is an internal, proprietary news automation and publishing tool. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle data in relation to our integration with Meta APIs (Facebook Graph API and Instagram Graph API).

## 2. Information Collection and Use
This application operates strictly as an internal automation tool. It does not collect, store, or process personal data from public users or consumers. 

The application utilizes Meta's Graph API exclusively to:
- Publish content (images, videos, captions, and hashtags) directly to the affiliated @cipherbrief Instagram and Facebook Pages.
- Read metadata associated with our own pages and publishing activities.

## 3. Data Sharing and Disclosure
We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer any information to outside parties. All data processed by this application remains within our internal infrastructure and the official Meta platforms it interacts with.

## 4. Third-Party Services
This application integrates with Meta's APIs. Your interaction with content published by this application on Meta platforms is governed by Meta's respective Privacy Policies (Facebook and Instagram).

## 5. Contact
For any questions regarding this privacy policy or the internal operations of the CipherBrief Newsroom application, please contact the repository administrator.
